Our Certification Process

1

Application & Documentation

Submit your application through our online portal with required documentation. Our team reviews your submission and contacts you within 3-5 business days to discuss your specific needs and requirements.

2

Initial Assessment

Our certification team conducts a preliminary assessment of your facility, processes, and documentation. We identify potential compliance gaps and provide recommendations for meeting Halal requirements.

3

On-Site Audit

A qualified HCA auditor visits your facility to verify compliance with Halal standards. The audit includes inspection of production areas, equipment, storage facilities, and review of processes and documentation.

4

Laboratory Testing

If required, samples are collected for laboratory analysis to verify the absence of non-Halal ingredients or contaminants. We work with accredited laboratories for accurate testing results.

5

Certification Decision

Our Shariah Board reviews the audit findings and makes a certification decision. If approved, you receive your Halal certificate valid for one year with our official HCA logo for use on products and marketing materials.

6

Ongoing Monitoring

We conduct regular surveillance audits to ensure continued compliance. Our team provides support for certificate renewal, process changes, and market expansion throughout the certification period.
