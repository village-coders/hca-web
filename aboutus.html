Leading Halal Certification in Nigeria

The Halal Certification Authority (HCA) Nigeria is the premier Halal certification body in Nigeria, established to provide authentic, reliable, and internationally recognized Halal certification services. We ensure that products, services, and establishments comply with Islamic Shariah law and are permissible (Halal) for Muslims to consume or use.

With Nigeria's significant Muslim population and growing demand for Halal products both locally and internationally, HCA Nigeria plays a crucial role in the Halal economy. We bridge the gap between Nigerian businesses and global Halal markets, facilitating trade while maintaining the highest standards of Halal integrity.

Our team of qualified Islamic scholars, food technologists, and certification experts work diligently to ensure that every product bearing the HCA Nigeria seal meets stringent Halal requirements from sourcing to final product.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide authentic, reliable, and internationally recognized Halal certification services that ensure compliance with Islamic Shariah law, promote consumer confidence, and facilitate access to global Halal markets for Nigerian businesses.

Our Vision

To be Nigeria's most trusted and authoritative Halal certification body, recognized globally for excellence in Halal standards and as a key enabler of Nigeria's participation in the global Halal economy.

Our Commitment

We are committed to upholding the highest standards of Halal integrity, providing transparent certification processes, and supporting Nigerian businesses in meeting both local and international Halal requirements.

Our Core Values

Integrity

We maintain the highest ethical standards in all our certification processes and decisions.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in service delivery, certification standards, and customer satisfaction.

Transparency

We ensure clear, open communication and transparent certification processes for all stakeholders.

Trust

We build and maintain trust through reliable certification and consistent application of Halal standards.

Our Journey

2015

Establishment of HCA Nigeria

HCA Nigeria was founded to address the growing need for authentic Halal certification in Nigeria's expanding Halal market.

2017

First International Recognition

Received recognition from key international Halal bodies, establishing credibility in global markets.

2019

Expansion of Services

Expanded certification services to include cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, and food service establishments.

2021

Digital Transformation

Launched online certification portal and digital certificate delivery system for improved efficiency.

2023

International Partnerships

Established strategic partnerships with Halal authorities in Saudi Arabia, UAE, and Malaysia.
