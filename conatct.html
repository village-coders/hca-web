Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the Halal certification process take?

The certification process typically takes 4-6 weeks from application to certificate issuance, depending on the complexity of your operation and the completeness of your documentation. Simple applications may be processed faster, while more complex facilities with multiple product lines may require additional time for thorough assessment.

What documents are required for Halal certification?

Required documents typically include: company registration documents, product list with ingredients, facility layout plan, supplier list with Halal certificates, manufacturing process flow, cleaning and sanitation procedures, and staff training records. Our team will provide you with a complete checklist specific to your business type during the application process.

How much does Halal certification cost?

Certification fees vary based on several factors including business size, number of products, complexity of operations, and whether you need domestic or international certification. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden costs. Contact our certification department for a personalized quote based on your specific needs.

Is HCA Nigeria certification recognized internationally?

Yes, HCA Nigeria certification is recognized in many countries worldwide, particularly in the Middle East, Southeast Asia, and Africa. We have mutual recognition agreements with several international Halal certification bodies. For specific country requirements, please contact our export certification department.

What happens after I get my Halal certificate?

After certification, we conduct regular surveillance audits (typically annually) to ensure ongoing compliance. We also provide support for certificate renewal, which is required yearly. Our team remains available to assist with any changes to your processes, products, or expansion plans that might affect your Halal certification status.

The Halal Certification Authority Nigeria is the leading body for Halal certification in Nigeria, ensuring products and services comply with Islamic Shariah law.
