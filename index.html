About Us

Who We Are

The Halal And Haram Distinction Development Initiative Nigeria (HDI) - Formally known as Halal certification Authority (HCA) is the premier and most trusted halal certification body in Nigeria. Established in 2005, we are dedicated to ensuring that products, services, and establishments comply with Islamic Shariah law and meet international halal standards.

Our team consists of Islamic scholars, food scientists, and certification experts who work together to provide comprehensive halal certification services. We are recognized by major international halal bodies and government agencies across the globe.

Our mission is to facilitate trade, protect Muslim consumers, and promote the halal industry in Nigeria while maintaining the highest standards of integrity and professionalism.

Successful applicants receive their halal certificate valid for one year (renewable).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Halal certification?
Halal certification is a process that ensures products, services, and establishments comply with Islamic Shariah law. It involves inspection of ingredients, production processes, storage, and handling to guarantee they meet halal standards.
How long does the certification process take?
The complete certification process typically takes 4-6 weeks from application submission to certificate issuance, depending on the complexity of the operation and completeness of documentation.
How much does Halal certification cost?
Certification fees vary based on the type of business, size of operation, and complexity. We offer transparent pricing with no hidden costs. Contact us for a customized quotation.
Is HDI certification recognized internationally?
Yes, HDI Nigeria is recognized by major international halal bodies including those in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Malaysia, Indonesia, and many other countries. Our certification facilitates export to Muslim-majority markets.
How long is the certificate valid?
Halal certificates are typically valid for one year. Renewal involves a simplified process including documentation review and periodic inspections to ensure ongoing compliance.
