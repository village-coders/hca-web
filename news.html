June 15, 2023

New Halal Standards for Food Processing in Nigeria

The Halal Certification Authority Nigeria has released updated guidelines for food processing facilities seeking Halal certification. These new standards align with international best practices and ensure higher quality control for consumers across Nigeria and export markets.

The Halal Certification Authority (HCA) Nigeria is pleased to announce the release of updated Halal standards for food processing facilities across the country. These comprehensive guidelines represent a significant step forward in ensuring the highest quality of Halal certification for Nigerian consumers and export markets.

Key Updates in the New Standards

  • Enhanced Traceability Requirements: All Halal-certified facilities must now implement comprehensive tracking systems for raw materials from source to finished product.
  • Improved Sanitation Protocols: New guidelines for cleaning and sanitation procedures to prevent cross-contamination with non-Halal products.
  • Staff Training Mandates: Mandatory Halal awareness training for all employees working in certified facilities.
  • Documentation Standards: Streamlined documentation requirements for easier compliance and auditing.

Implementation Timeline

The new standards will be implemented in phases over the next 12 months. Existing certified facilities will have until June 2024 to fully comply with all updated requirements. HCA Nigeria will provide training sessions and support materials to help businesses transition smoothly.

According to HCA Director General, Dr. Ahmed Ibrahim: "These updated standards reflect our commitment to excellence in Halal certification. By aligning with international best practices, we're ensuring Nigerian Halal products maintain the highest standards of quality and integrity in global markets."

For more information about the new standards or to download the complete guidelines, visit the HCA Nigeria website or contact our standards department at standards@halalcert.com.ng.

May 28, 2023

HCA Nigeria Signs MoU with Saudi Food and Drug Authority

In a landmark agreement, HCA Nigeria has signed a Memorandum of Understanding with the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA) to streamline Halal certification for Nigerian exporters. This partnership will facilitate easier access to Saudi markets for Nigerian Halal products.

The Halal Certification Authority Nigeria has entered into a historic partnership with the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA), marking a significant milestone in Nigeria's Halal export industry. The Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) was signed in Riyadh during a bilateral trade summit between Nigerian and Saudi officials.

Key Benefits of the Partnership

  • Streamlined Certification: Nigerian exporters will benefit from simplified certification processes for the Saudi market.
  • Reciprocal Recognition: HCA Nigeria-certified products will be recognized by SFDA, reducing duplicate certification requirements.
  • Capacity Building: Joint training programs for Nigerian Halal auditors and inspectors.
  • Market Access: Enhanced access to the lucrative Saudi market for Nigerian Halal food, cosmetics, and pharmaceutical products.

Economic Impact

This agreement is expected to significantly boost Nigeria's Halal exports to Saudi Arabia and other Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) countries. Currently valued at approximately $50 million annually, Nigerian Halal exports to the region are projected to increase by over 300% in the next five years as a result of this partnership.

HCA Nigeria Chairman, Alhaji Musa Bello, commented on the agreement: "This MoU represents a major step forward for Nigeria's Halal industry. By aligning our standards and processes with one of the world's most respected Halal authorities, we're opening doors for Nigerian businesses in the global Halal market."

The partnership also includes provisions for technical exchange programs, joint research initiatives, and collaboration on Halal standards development. Nigerian businesses interested in exporting to Saudi Arabia can now apply for streamlined certification through the HCA Nigeria website.

April 10, 2023

Successful Halal Awareness Workshop in Lagos

HCA Nigeria completed a successful three-day Halal certification workshop for restaurants and food service providers in Lagos state. Over 150 participants gained valuable insights into Halal compliance requirements and certification processes.

The Halal Certification Authority Nigeria recently concluded a highly successful three-day Halal awareness and certification workshop at the Lagos Continental Hotel. The event attracted over 150 participants from restaurants, hotels, catering services, and food processing companies across Lagos State.

Workshop Highlights

  • Day 1: Fundamentals of Halal certification, understanding Shariah requirements, and basic compliance standards.
  • Day 2: Practical implementation of Halal standards in food service environments, including kitchen layout, equipment, and staff training.
  • Day 3: Certification process walkthrough, documentation requirements, and post-certification compliance monitoring.

Participant Feedback

Participants praised the workshop for its practical approach and comprehensive coverage of Halal certification requirements. Mrs. Amina Yusuf, owner of a popular Lagos restaurant chain, shared her experience: "This workshop has been eye-opening. We've been serving Halal food for years, but now we understand the full certification process and how to ensure complete compliance."

The workshop also featured a practical demonstration session where participants learned proper Halal food handling techniques, cross-contamination prevention, and Halal supply chain management.

Upcoming Workshops

Due to high demand, HCA Nigeria has announced additional workshops in Abuja (June 2023), Kano (July 2023), and Port Harcourt (August 2023). Interested businesses can register through the HCA Nigeria website or contact the training department at training@halalcert.com.ng.

HCA Nigeria Training Director, Hajiya Fatima Abdul, noted: "The enthusiastic response to our Lagos workshop demonstrates the growing awareness and importance of proper Halal certification in Nigeria's food service industry. We're committed to supporting businesses through these educational initiatives."

March 22, 2023

New Online Certification Portal Launched

HCA Nigeria has launched a new online portal for easier application and tracking of Halal certification requests. The portal features a streamlined application process, real-time status updates, and digital certificate delivery.

HCA Nigeria has officially launched its new digital certification portal, marking a significant advancement in the organization's service delivery. The portal, accessible at portal.halalcert.com.ng, offers a completely digitalized certification process from application to certificate issuance.

Portal Features

  • Online Application: Complete the entire certification application online with guided step-by-step process.
  • Document Upload: Securely upload all required documentation directly through the portal.
  • Real-time Tracking: Monitor your application status 24/7 with detailed progress updates.
  • Digital Certificates: Receive your Halal certificate digitally with secure verification features.
  • Renewal Reminders: Automated notifications for certificate renewals and updates.

Benefits for Businesses

The new portal significantly reduces processing time for Halal certification applications. What previously took 4-6 weeks can now be completed in 2-3 weeks for standard applications. The portal also reduces paperwork and provides a more transparent process for applicants.

Mr. Tunde Oke, CEO of a Lagos-based food processing company, was among the first to use the new portal: "The process was incredibly smooth. I submitted our application on Monday, uploaded all documents by Wednesday, and we're already scheduled for inspection next week. The transparency is a game-changer."

Security and Support

The portal features enterprise-level security with data encryption and secure authentication. A dedicated support team is available to assist users with the new system via email, phone, and live chat during business hours.

HCA Nigeria IT Director, Engr. Chinedu Okoro, emphasized: "This portal represents our commitment to leveraging technology for better service delivery. We've designed it with user experience in mind, making Halal certification more accessible and efficient for all Nigerian businesses."

Existing HCA Nigeria clients can migrate their accounts to the new portal, while new users can register directly on the platform. Training materials and video tutorials are available on the HCA Nigeria website.

February 18, 2023

HCA Hosts Export Seminar for Nigerian Food Producers

The Halal Certification Authority organized a specialized seminar for Nigerian food producers looking to export to Middle Eastern markets. The event covered certification requirements, market entry strategies, and compliance with international Halal standards.

HCA Nigeria recently hosted a comprehensive export seminar targeting Nigerian food producers interested in accessing Middle Eastern markets. The event, held at the Transcorp Hilton Abuja, brought together over 200 business owners, export managers, and agricultural producers from across Nigeria.

Seminar Topics Covered

  • Market Overview: Understanding Halal market opportunities in the Middle East, with focus on UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and Kuwait.
  • Certification Requirements: Detailed breakdown of Halal certification requirements for different product categories.
  • Export Documentation: Complete guide to export documentation, customs clearance, and logistics for Halal products.
  • Quality Standards: Meeting international quality and safety standards alongside Halal requirements.
  • Success Stories: Case studies from Nigerian companies successfully exporting Halal products to the region.

Expert Presentations

The seminar featured presentations from international Halal experts, trade specialists from the Nigerian Export Promotion Council, and successful Nigerian exporters. A key highlight was a virtual presentation from Dubai-based Halal industry consultant, Dr. Omar Al-Mansoori, who shared insights on Middle Eastern consumer preferences and market trends.

Alhaji Ibrahim Suleiman, whose company exports processed cashew nuts to Saudi Arabia, shared his experience: "This seminar provided exactly the information we needed. The connections made and knowledge gained will help us expand to three new markets this year."

Follow-up Support

HCA Nigeria has announced ongoing support for seminar participants, including:

  • One-on-one consultation sessions for developing export strategies
  • Assistance with product formulation to meet target market preferences
  • Introductions to potential buyers and distributors in Middle Eastern markets
  • Priority processing for export certification applications

The success of this seminar has prompted HCA Nigeria to plan similar events focused on specific product categories, including dairy, meat, and processed foods. Interested businesses can join the HCA Nigeria Export Development Program for continuous support.
